# ☕ Support Us

If the resources and notes on this site are helpful to you, voluntary support is greatly appreciated. 🙏

> All mirrors, scripts and tutorials on this site are fully free to access, with no paywalls.
> Site maintenance, speed testing and resource updates are done in spare time.
> Donations are completely optional and unobligated. You can use all content normally whether you support or not.

## Why Your Support Matters

Maintaining and organizing resources takes a lot of spare time:

- **Resource curation**: Collecting various tools, mirrors and practical scripts, continuous validation and updates
- **Documentation writing**: Recording technical practices and polishing guides
- **Site development**: Page iteration, component improvements and issue handling
- **Site overhead**: Domain, CDN, automated build and other related costs

I regularly batch-test all mirror sources to remove broken links and keep them usable. This repetitive work requires constant network verification. Your support keeps this project going.
